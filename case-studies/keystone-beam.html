Keystone & Beam

Contractor Website | Demonstration Project

View Keystone & Beam demo

Business Overview

Project Overview

Keystone & Beam is a demonstration website independently designed and developed by ADK Digital to showcase a professional online presence for a residential contracting business. The project emphasizes visual presentation, clear service descriptions, and an organized portfolio of completed work.

The Challenge

For contractors, a website needs to do more than list available services. Potential customers often want to see examples of previous work, understand the company's specialties, and determine whether the business is a good fit for their project.

The design challenge was to organize this information into an attractive, easy-to-navigate website that communicates professionalism and makes it convenient for prospective customers to request additional information.

Our Solution

ADK Digital developed a responsive contractor website that combines service descriptions, project imagery, company information, and customer inquiry functionality.

The design uses a professional visual style to emphasize craftsmanship and attention to detail. A project gallery allows visitors to explore examples of work, while dedicated service and contact sections help guide potential customers toward making an inquiry.

Key Features

  • Service Listings: Clearly organized descriptions help visitors understand the contractor's areas of expertise.
  • Project Gallery: An interactive gallery with a lightbox allows visitors to examine examples of construction and renovation work.
  • Company Information: An About section introduces the hypothetical business and its approach to contracting.
  • Testimonial Carousel: An interactive component demonstrates how customer feedback could be presented.
  • Contact Form: A form with input validation demonstrates how prospective customers could submit project inquiries.
  • Responsive Design: The website adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Business Value

Although Keystone & Beam is a demonstration project rather than a commissioned website, it illustrates how a well-organized contractor website could:

  • Help potential customers identify relevant services.
  • Showcase craftsmanship through project imagery.
  • Establish a professional and consistent online presence.
  • Make it easier for prospective customers to initiate contact.
  • Present testimonials and project examples in an accessible format.

These are intended benefits of the design rather than verified business outcomes.

Technical Details

Keystone & Beam was developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The website includes responsive layouts, an interactive project gallery, a testimonial carousel, and client-side contact form validation.

The business, project examples, and testimonials are demonstration content. The contact interface does not represent a verified production inquiry system.

Project Disclosure

Keystone & Beam is a fictional contracting business website created independently by ADK Digital to demonstrate its web design and development capabilities. It does not represent a commissioned client project.
